# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We are committed to a welcoming, respectful, and constructive project community, regardless of
experience, background, identity, or ability.

## Expected behavior

- Be considerate and specific when giving feedback.
- Critique ideas and changes, not people.
- Assume good intent while remaining accountable for impact.
- Respect privacy and never ask contributors to publish sensitive datasets or personal details.
- Accept reasonable maintainer decisions about project scope, safety, and release readiness.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, threats, discriminatory language, sexualized attention, sustained disruption,
doxxing, or publishing another person's private information without permission are not accepted
in project spaces.

## Enforcement

Repository maintainers may edit or remove contributions and may temporarily or permanently limit
participation when behavior conflicts with this policy. Enforcement decisions should be
proportionate, documented privately when practical, and avoid unnecessary disclosure.

For a private conduct report, use a private contact method listed on the
[@internalforces GitHub profile](https://github.com/internalforces). Do not open a public issue
that contains personal or sensitive information. Immediate threats may also be reported through
[GitHub's abuse-reporting process](https://support.github.com/contact/report-abuse).

This policy applies in repository issues, pull requests, reviews, discussions, release spaces,
and other project-hosted communication.
