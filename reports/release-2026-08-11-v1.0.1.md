## Platform trust boundary

- macOS: Apple Silicon only; ad-hoc integrity signature only; no Developer ID signature or Apple
  notarization. Gatekeeper may warn or block the app on another Mac.
- Windows: x64 only; the executable and MSI are not Authenticode-signed. SmartScreen or Smart App
  Control may warn or block them on another PC.
- The packages are local desktop software. Analysis remains local and no dataset is sent to an
  external service.

These boundaries are disclosed in the v1.0.1 Release notes and remain tracked by TD-007/TD-008.
